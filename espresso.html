Reflect on Round 1

  • What made you pause? Words you reached for and missed, sentence starts you abandoned.
  • What did you say wrong? Verb tense, article, collocation, pronunciation that you noticed mid-sentence.
  • What did you miss? Points you meant to cover but ran out of time or forgot.

Tighten the structure

  • Cut unimportant details. Every example you keep should earn its place.
  • Adjust the structure. Lead with the strongest point — don't bury the lede.
  • Land the closer. A short, punchy sentence beats a trailing one.
