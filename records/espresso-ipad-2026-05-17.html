Section 03

Final Speech vs. AI-Improved Version

My Final Speech (Round 3)

iPad is the a piece of technology that quietly changed my life in two perspectives. First, studying. I like to take notes. Before, i take notes on papers. But they are hard to keep and store. Now I use iPad with Apple Pencil to take digital notes. It becomes easy to modify my notes and put pictures in them. The most important thing is, they all store in my iPad. The second perspective is entertainment. When I travel I watch videos through laptop in my phone. But the screen is too small. Now I bring my iPad with me, it provides a better video-watching experience.

AI-Improved Version

The iPad is a piece of technology that has quietly changed my life in two ways: how I study and how I enjoy entertainment.
First, studying. I love taking notes, but paper notes were always hard to organize and store. With the iPad and Apple Pencil, I now take digital notes that are easy to edit, allow me to insert images, and most importantly, stay neatly stored in one place.
Second, entertainment. I often watch videos while traveling. I used to rely on my phone, but the screen was too small to enjoy. Now I bring my iPad along, and it gives me a much better viewing experience.
In both studying and entertainment, the iPad has made everyday moments more convenient and enjoyable.
